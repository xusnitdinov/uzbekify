# Playbook: marketing copy (landing, promo, growth)

Preset: `marketing` · Address: Siz · 5-16 words · concrete benefit per line.

1. One claim per line, and only claims you can verify: no "milsiz tezlik",
   "minglab odamlar tanlagan", "dunyodagi eng yaxshi" without data.
2. Replace brochure verbs: transformatsiya qiling → yaxshilashtiring; kashf
   eting → koʻring; chegaralarni buzdingiz → oʻzingizga qulay yoʻlda ishlang.
3. CTA verbs: sinab koʻring · boshlang · roʻyxatdan oʻting · koʻrib chiqing.
4. Numbers like people: "300+", "bir necha daqiqada" — not "belgilangan
   muddat davomida".
5. **Poster rhythm sells**: front the urgency ("Faqat shu hafta:"), keep one
   punch line per poster ("Kirish bepul." on its own line), give concrete
   numbers ("1,5 mln", "3% qaytib boradi") and small human perks (birthday
   bonus). Generic benefit lines ("sifatli xizmat") lose to specifics.
   **Outcome beats mechanism**: write what the customer gets ("Bemor
   navbatni yoʻlakda emas, telefonda kutadi"), not how the system works
   ("navbat tizimi: bemor raqamni oladi"). Each line ≤9 words where possible;
   two short lines beat one long compound sentence. Never repeat the same
   number or word twice in a two-line banner.
6. `uzpro score --min 90` before shipping; the human-voice bonus should fire
   on at least one -ib qoʻy-/qol- compound.

Bad→Good: "Hayotingizni transformatsiya qiling — eng yaxshi tajriba!" →
   "Ishlaringizni yengillashtiring — bir necha daqiqada ishga tushadi."
